Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Which companies is your advisory service for?

We focus on innovative, technology-focused startup teams and companies. We support projects from the early idea through to an approved funding project. Your location, company stage and project determine which programmes may be suitable.

Which funding routes do you assess?

We assess the funding areas described above based on your location, company stage and specific project. This determines which programmes are currently suitable.

Do you also take on projects that have already been approved?

Yes. We support approved projects with financial administration, personnel records, reports, audits and formal project completion. The exact scope depends on the programme and project.

Do you guarantee funding approval?

Funding approval cannot be guaranteed. We provide a realistic assessment of your chances and improve the quality, clarity and funding rationale of your application. If the prospects are poor, we tell you early.

How are your services priced?

Depending on complexity, we agree a small initial fee and a success fee based on the amount of funding actually approved. If funding is approved, the initial fee is credited in full. We price ongoing project support according to the work involved. Before we begin, you receive a clear example calculation.
